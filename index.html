Where your words go

Your words stay on this device. Here is every stop, plainly.

Your browser turns speech into text. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to do this; Safari uses Apple’s engine. With “Hear everyone on a call” on, the other people’s voices go there too. Vaani itself never receives your audio.
Notes stay on this device. Everything you save lives in this browser’s storage (local storage, or IndexedDB when that is full). Clearing browser data deletes it.
The built-in notes engine runs on your device. If you add your own AI key in Settings, text (never audio) goes to that provider whenever Vaani writes notes, answers a question, recaps your week, translates or drafts a follow-up. Ask and Recap send the notes they search.
Google Calendar, only if you connect it. Vaani reads your next 36 hours of events straight from Google into this browser. None of it goes to a Vaani account or database. Disconnect any time. Attendee names stay on this device; an event’s title becomes the note’s title, which goes to your AI provider like any note title if you use your own key. A note you take for an event keeps the attendee names; backups leave them out unless you turn them on in Settings. Disconnect deletes the saved events and asks Google to remove Vaani’s access. If Google can’t, Vaani tells you, and you can remove it in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
