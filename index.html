Where your words go

Vaani has no server and no account. Here is every stop, plainly.

Your browser turns speech into text. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to do this; Safari uses Apple's engine. Vaani itself never receives your audio.
Notes stay on this device. Everything you save lives in this browser only. Clearing browser data deletes it.
The built-in notes engine runs on your device. If you add your own AI key in Settings, the text (never audio) goes to that provider when notes are made.
Tell people you are taking notes. It's polite, and in many places it's the law.
