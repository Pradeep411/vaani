Use Sarvam AI for speech to text?

Sarvam AI can turn speech into text with your key. Your meeting audio and your Names and words list then go from this browser to Sarvam AI. When Vaani hears a call through its Chrome extension, other people’s voices go too. Nothing goes to Vaani.

Where your words go

Your notes stay on this device. Here is every stop, plainly.

Speech to text. Without a Sarvam AI key, your browser does it: Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft; Safari uses Apple’s engine. With a Sarvam AI key, the meeting audio goes from this browser straight to Sarvam AI, in pieces of about 25 seconds, to be turned into text, together with your Names and words list so names come out right. When Vaani hears a call through its Chrome extension, the other people’s voices go there too. Nothing goes to Vaani: the audio is held only in memory and dropped once it is transcribed.
Notes stay on this device. Everything you save lives in this browser’s storage (local storage, or IndexedDB when that is full). Clearing browser data deletes it.
The built-in notes engine runs on your device. If you add your own AI key in Settings, text goes to that provider whenever Vaani writes notes, answers a question, recaps your week, translates or drafts a follow-up. Ask and Recap send the notes they search. OpenAI, Claude and Gemini get text only; a Sarvam AI key also turns on Sarvam AI’s speech to text, so Sarvam AI gets the audio too.
Google Calendar, only if you connect it. Vaani reads your next 36 hours of events straight from Google into this browser. None of it goes to a Vaani account or database. Disconnect any time. Attendee names and email addresses stay on this device; the addresses only fill in the To line of a follow-up email, or the guests of a follow-up meeting, when you tap. An event’s title becomes the note’s title, which goes to your AI provider like any note title if you use your own key. A note you take for an event keeps the attendee names and addresses; backups leave them out unless you turn them on in Settings. Disconnect deletes the saved events and asks Google to remove Vaani’s access. If Google can’t, Vaani tells you, and you can remove it in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
After a meeting. Vaani drafts the follow-up email, the tasks and the follow-up meeting in this browser. Gmail, your mail app, WhatsApp and Google Calendar open only when you tap, filled in for you to check and send. Slack gets the notes only if you add a Slack channel link in Settings: when you tap, or on its own after each meeting if you choose “Do it automatically”.
Tell people you are taking notes. It’s polite, and in many places it’s the law.
