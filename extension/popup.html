Vaani, your AI note taker agent

Vaani spots Google Meet, Teams and Zoom calls in Chrome and offers to take the notes. You can also start on any tab with a call.

Let Vaani hear this call?

To hear everyone, not only you, Vaani needs Chrome’s OK once. Chrome’s message says “Read and change all your data on all websites”. Vaani uses it only to hear a call tab you start notes on, until you stop.

With a Sarvam AI key, everyone’s words become text. Without one, Vaani keeps the call’s audio in its window, so you can make notes from it later.
