Privacy

Vaani web app and Vaani Chrome extension. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Short version: Vaani has no account, no server and no tracking. Your notes stay in your browser.

The Chrome extension

  • On Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom web pages, it checks whether a “Leave call” button is showing, so it knows a call has started. It does not read your messages, names, video or anything else on the page.
  • When you click Start notes, it opens the Vaani app in a small window. Nothing is sent anywhere by the extension.
  • It stores one setting in Chrome: whether to ask you when a call starts.
  • It does not record audio by itself. Recording happens in the Vaani app, only after you press the record button.

The Vaani app

  • Speech to text uses your browser’s speech engine. In Chrome and Edge the browser sends the audio to Google or Microsoft to do this. Safari uses Apple’s engine. Vaani never receives your audio.
  • Hear everyone on a call: if you turn this on and share a call’s tab, its sound is mixed with your microphone inside your browser and passed to the same speech engine, so the other people’s voices go there too. Nothing is saved as audio.
  • Notes are written on your device by default. If you add your own AI key (Sarvam AI, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), text, never audio, goes to that provider whenever Vaani writes notes, answers a question, recaps your week, translates or drafts a follow-up. Ask and Recap send the notes they search. Your key is stored only in your browser.
  • Saved notes live only in this browser’s storage (local storage, or IndexedDB when that is full). Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Insights are worked out in your browser from the notes you saved. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it: Vaani reads your next 36 hours of events straight from Google into this browser. Nothing goes to any Vaani server. Disconnect any time. Access is read-only and the token is kept in memory only. Titles, times and call links are kept here for 36 hours, and removed the next time you open Vaani after that. Attendee names stay on this device. A note you take for an event keeps its title, which goes to your AI provider like any note title if you use your own key. That note also keeps the event’s attendee names and event id; backups leave both out unless you turn on “Include attendee names in backups” in Settings. Disconnecting deletes the saved events and asks Google to remove Vaani’s access. If the sign-in has expired, Vaani asks Google for a short-lived token only to revoke it. If Google can’t do that without your sign-in, Vaani tells you, and you can remove access yourself in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections (myaccount.google.com/connections).
  • No accounts, cookies for tracking, analytics or ads.

Your choices

  • Turn off the call prompt in the extension’s menu, or remove the extension at any time.
  • Delete any note from inside Vaani.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings at any time. You can also remove Vaani in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections (myaccount.google.com/connections).
